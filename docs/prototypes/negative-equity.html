Worth understanding before you do this

Carrying negative equity into a new agreement means you start the new car owing more than it is worth. That is not a reason not to do it, and for most people changing car is the right call, but you should know it rather than find out in two years.

Two things shorten it: putting cash in now, and choosing a car that holds its value. A shorter term on the settlement loan costs more each month and far less overall.

Before you apply

Covering negative equity needs a strong credit history, and there are two agreements, so the lender will run two full credit searches. Both leave a footprint on your file that other lenders can see, whether you are accepted or not.

If you are not sure where you stand, check your rate first. That is a soft search and leaves no mark.
