6 About the menu

Why the menu feels busy: each of the five items opens a large panel with quick links, highlighted pages and questions. Cars and Finance both repeat the same monthly price links.

Where it came from: this structure was set up by the build team while developing the site. It was not based on a signed-off site map.

Next step: once the site map is agreed, the menu can be simplified to match.
